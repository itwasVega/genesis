# Genesis for iPhone

Send notes to Genesis from your seat: "missed class", "test moved to Friday", "quiz covers 4.2". Fire off as many as you want; Genesis puts them together and reshapes your plan before you're back at your Mac. There's nothing to set up: the first note you send is all it takes.

## Get the beta

The iPhone app is in beta on Apple's TestFlight.

1. Install [TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) from the App Store.
2. **Join the Genesis beta:** the public TestFlight link will be posted right here as soon as Apple approves the beta. Until then, [request an invite](https://github.com/itwasVega/genesis/discussions) and you'll get one by email.
3. Open Genesis on your iPhone and send your first note. Your Mac picks it up within a minute.

## How notes travel

Your iPhone saves each note to a **Genesis** folder in your iCloud Drive, and Genesis on your Mac reads it from there. Notes never pass through anyone else's server. See [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md).
