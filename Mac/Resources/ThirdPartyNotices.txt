Genesis includes the following third-party components.

Python 3.12.14, from python-build-standalone via the local runtime bundle:
  Python/lib/python3.12/LICENSE.txt
  PythonDependencyLicenses.txt (upstream dependency license collection)
  https://github.com/astral-sh/python-build-standalone
The collection includes notices for optional upstream components; its inclusion
does not mean every listed component is present. The packaged interpreter includes
OpenSSL, SQLite, libffi, compression libraries, libedit, Tcl/Tk and timezone data.

Calendar parsing dependencies (icalendar, python-dateutil, six, tzdata):
  Backend/outputs/vendor/calendar/*dist-info/ (licenses retained per package)

KaTeX and associated fonts:
  Math/LICENSE and Math/fonts/ (MIT license notices retained)

Instrument Serif, Copyright 2022 The Instrument Serif Project Authors:
  Fonts/OFL.txt (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

PDF extraction and OCR use Apple's PDFKit and Vision frameworks supplied by macOS.
PyMuPDF and the developer's previous private font files are not part of the app.

Codex is installed and authenticated separately by the user. It is not bundled.
